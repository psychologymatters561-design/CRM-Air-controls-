Air Control's 9-Point AC Maintenance Protocol

Every Air Control service visit follows a documented 9-step protocol — not a quick clean-and-go. You receive a signed service checklist at the end of every visit.

  1. Filter Inspection and Cleaning

    Remove and inspect air filters. Wash reusable filters with high-pressure water; replace disposable filters. Clogged filters reduce airflow by up to 40% and are the leading cause of premature compressor failure.

  2. Evaporator Coil Cleaning

    Apply no-rinse coil cleaner to the indoor evaporator coil. Remove dust, mould, and microbial buildup that reduces heat exchange efficiency and degrades indoor air quality.

  3. Condenser Coil Cleaning

    Flush the outdoor condenser coil with water and coil cleaner to clear debris and oxidation. A dirty condenser forces the compressor to work harder, raising electricity consumption by 10–30%.

  4. Refrigerant Pressure Check

    Connect pressure gauges to measure refrigerant charge. Top up to manufacturer specification if required. Under- or over-charged systems lose cooling capacity and shorten compressor life.

  5. Drain Pan and Condensate Line Flush

    Flush the condensate drain pan and line to prevent algae and bacterial blockage — the most common cause of water overflow onto ceilings and walls.

  6. Electrical Connections and Safety Check

    Inspect and tighten all electrical terminals. Check capacitors, contactors, and wiring for wear, corrosion, or heat damage. Loose connections are a fire risk.

  7. Blower Motor and Fan Blade Inspection

    Check blower motor amperage draw, inspect fan blades for cracks or balance defects, and lubricate bearings. Imbalanced fans cause vibration damage and escalating noise.

  8. Thermostat Calibration

    Verify thermostat accuracy and recalibrate if out of tolerance. A mis-calibrated thermostat causes the system to short-cycle or run continuously — wasting electricity.

  9. Full System Performance Test and Sign-Off

    Run the system in cooling mode. Measure supply and return air temperature differential, airflow velocity, and noise. Record all readings in the service log and issue a signed checklist to the customer.
