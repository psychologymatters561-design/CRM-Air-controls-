Our service standard

Air Control's 9-Point Maintenance Protocol

Every visit. Every time. You receive a signed service checklist at the end of each visit.

1

Filter Inspection & Cleaning

Wash reusable filters with high-pressure water. Replace disposable filters. Clogged filters cause 40% airflow loss.

2

Evaporator Coil Cleaning

No-rinse coil cleaner removes mould and microbial buildup from the indoor unit.

3

Condenser Coil Cleaning

Outdoor unit flushed to clear debris and oxidation — dirty condensers raise power consumption 10–30%.

4

Refrigerant Pressure Check

Gauged to manufacturer spec. Topped up if required. Under-charged systems damage the compressor.

5

Drain Pan & Line Flush

Condensate drain cleared with cleaning solution to prevent overflow and ceiling water damage.

6

Electrical Safety Check

All terminals tightened. Capacitors, contactors, and wiring checked for heat damage or corrosion.

7

Blower Motor & Fan Inspection

Amperage checked. Blades inspected for cracks or imbalance. Bearings lubricated.

8

Thermostat Calibration

Verified against calibrated thermometer. Recalibrated if out of tolerance to prevent short-cycling.

9

Full Performance Test & Sign-Off

Cooling mode run. Temperature differential, airflow, and noise recorded. Signed checklist issued.
